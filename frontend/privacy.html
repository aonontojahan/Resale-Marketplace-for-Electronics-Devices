Privacy Policy

Your trust is our most valuable asset. Learn exactly how we handle your information with transparency and care.

1. Introduction

ReSale ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity Data: Full name, username, and initials.
  • Contact Data: Email address and phone number (for delivery purposes).
  • Transaction Data: Details of products bought/sold, prices, and escrow status.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and operating system (for fraud prevention).
  • Communication Data: Chat logs within the platform are stored for dispute resolution purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To facilitate transactions and verify escrow payments.
  • To resolve disputes between buyers and sellers.
  • To prevent fraud and maintain the security of our community.
  • To provide customer support via WhatsApp or Email.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share necessary information with:

  • Transaction Counterparties: Sellers receive buyer delivery info; Buyers see seller business names.
  • Service Providers: Payment processors (e.g., bKash gateways) and hosting providers.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when required by law to report fraudulent activity or stolen property.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for all data in transit. Passwords are cryptographically hashed and never stored in plain text.

6. Your Rights

Under modern data protection standards, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your session preferences. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy-related concerns, please contact our Data Protection Officer at: aonontojahan@gmail.com.
